On 2026-10-22 the owner set a target: **finish the Skylark MVP before 2027**, so about 10 weeks from that date.

The owner's core loop:
1. Own Quins.
2. Register for an arena event.
3. Wait in a 1-minute prep room.
4. Spectate from a bench, the arena cameras or a freecam.
5. The event ends.
6. The Quin needs maintenance.
7. Repair it at the Quin Workshop or with a self-repair kit.
8. Repeat.

**Schedule:**
- Source: `D:\SKYLARK\ARENA SCHEDULE.png`. One Skylark day = 8 real hours, 3 cycles a day, pinned to UTC.
- Owner's worries: the schedule is too packed; players miss events or wait too long. They want it balanced.
- They are also considering 2-3 arenas.
- They want 4 seasons: spring, summer, fall, winter.

**My proposals (2026-10-22), not decided by the owner:**
- An event is a window of repeating rounds, not one match.
- Players who sat out a round get priority next round.
- NPQs fill empty slots, so a round always starts on time.
- Rest (00-06 SST) stops official events only; the arena stays open, and Rest is repair time.
- Seasons: 1 season = 3 Skylark weeks = exactly 1 real week, so a year = 4 real weeks.
- One arena for the MVP; a side arena later.
- MVP cuts: no ranked ladders, championships, Create Your Quin or accessories.

**Why:** this is the deadline that scopes everything. Keep the owner on MVP scope (see [[owner-overwhelm-small-steps]]).

**How to apply:** check new feature asks against the MVP loop. Park anything outside it, and say so.
