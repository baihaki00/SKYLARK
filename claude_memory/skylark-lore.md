On 2026-10-19 the owner pasted a ChatGPT "project bible" for Skylark Isles. The owner said to ignore its Quin spec (AI attributes, elements and baseplates, the four-Quin stuff) and focus on **lore**. Their target: Attack on Titan-level depth, "godly writing", mind-twisting.

**Established in the owner's world (names and setting only):**
- floating islands and airships; the city of Argonia (the player arrives by flying ship);
- the Architect (mythic figure); the Great Reset (historic event); KIBA Technologies Inc.; Jerim (character);
- the Grand Arena; Quins are autonomous, do not speak, have mysterious "Quin sounds", and look like a faceless mannequin/marionette;
- the Respect Custom; Pack Leaders; a newspaper that records history.

**Unknown canon:** the Architect, the Great Reset, KIBA, Jerim, Quin origins, why the islands float. Don't invent these as fact.

**Claude's first proposal (2026-10-19), "the registration twist", NOT canon:**
- Quins built the islands and humans erased them in the Great Reset.
- "Manufacturing" is really a reset of a finite population, and registration wipes memory.
- Faceless by law.
- The Respect Custom is a Quin rite that humans turned into sport.
- The Glitcher is a Quin whose wipe failed.
- The opening line "The Architect built the islands. We built the Quins." is the lie.

Await the owner's pick.

**How to apply:** label proposals as proposals. Tie lore twists to game mechanics, so play becomes evidence. Be honest about clichés and derivativeness (AoT, Westworld, NieR, Detroit).
