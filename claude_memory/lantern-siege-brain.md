**Step 1 built (commit 815723a):** Cognition/Callouts: a Quin seeing an enemy that is news (no track, or own track stale > CalloutNewAfter 4 s) may shout (chance 0.35 + 0.5 x awareness, cooldown 2.5 s, team won't recall same enemy for 4 s); allies within CalloutRange 110 of the shout get a report track (heardFrom); caller looks at the enemy, hearers glance (0.6). Attrs CalloutOf/CalloutAt, HeardCalloutOf/From/At; mind panel lines; Tracks overlay "(X called it)". Layer switch Cognition_Callouts. Measured 16v16: ~2.2 calls per Quin per min, no errors; known-enemies on vs off not measurable in the open 16v16 (Quins already know ~10-15 of 16) - its value shows with broken sight lines / fog (Lantern Siege).
