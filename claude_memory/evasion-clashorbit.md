**Modules/Evasion** (new): focused dash (Tactics.FocusDash*, 4 m = 18.5 studs, timed by DashStart/DashEnd/Recover markers, faces target, arcs) used in Circling when backing off a close target and in Fight round a guard; quick evade (Tactics.QuickEvade*) from Instinct's slip: attacker's StrikeSide (FightState) Left -> evade Right, Right -> Left, Back either; mistakes ~0.2*(1.5-awareness) lean into it; DamageModule returns "Dodged" via Evasion.slips. Instinct_SlipChance 0.15 -> 0.3. Cascadeur m -> studs: x4.63 (hip 3.93 studs / 0.849 m).

**Circling**: close target -> keeps facing it and backpedals (Circling_TurnBackChance 0.25 still turns). Back-turned samples 82 -> 35.

**Fixes**: slam shockwave only enemies, knockdown <=6 studs (SlamKnockdownRadius), rest shoved; ReEntry no more per-tick CFrame writes (ReEntryFacing align); DecisionSystem "EvenlyHurt": low HP vs equally hurt 1v1 no retreat (endgame ping-pong reproduced, fix NOT retested); StopRun needs StopRun_MinTopSpeedTime 0.45 s at top speed, cut by a strike within 0.35 s (LocomotionModule.cutStopRun) - NOT tested.

**Two styles (2026-10-27)**: ClashOrbit.newSim(style,...) -> "Dogfight" (main) or "Leap" (MidAirClash_OrbitLeapChance 0.25), published as OrbitStyle. Dogfight = the crossing dogfight restored + polished: Hermite curves timed by physics (speed = falling-body speed by height on each curve, slowest at loop top, fastest diving, + DogfightKick fading out of a hit, then scaled so both arrive at T; gEff = g/scale^2 for the limbs), rise out of a hit / dive into the next, body = nose along flight (pitch <=55) + up along felt pull (acc*Lean + g, bank <=70), hit reel-back (25-55 deg, recovers 0.12 s) or backflip 25%; DogfightTimeScale 0.75.
