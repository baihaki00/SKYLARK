Improve Quins in general, never as a new mode-specific thing. Movement, pacing, commitment, gaze, gestures, eyes, body language: all of it is built in the shared QuinCore systems (TaskChoice/Main/States/GaitModule/LocomotionModule/Cognition), and it must show up in the 16v16 flagship as much as in Lantern Siege. A mode (Lantern Siege) only adds objectives: things to want and places to be. It never adds its own Quin behaviour.

**Why:** the owner said: "whatever changes must reflect our 16vs16 flagship... improve quins in general do not improve them as a whole new thing". Quins are one living creature ([[quin-creature-vision]]), and the 16v16 is the flagship.

**How to apply:**
- Before building, ask whether this fix works in the 16v16. If it only fires when Objectives.active(), it's wrong, unless it is purely an objective (where to go, what to want).
- Siege-only things allowed: the arena look and layout, the huddle presentation, the map screen.
- Test every Quin change in the 16v16 too (protect the 16v16; compare old vs new with a switch). See [[lantern-siege-brain]], [[quin-momentum]].
