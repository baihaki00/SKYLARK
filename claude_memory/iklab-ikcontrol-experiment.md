On 2026-10-03 the owner asked to experiment with Roblox IKControl + constraints on a Quin, outside QuinCore ("don't touch quincore"), to decide between IK/procedural and animation-only (they compared with Unreal's GASP, 500+ clips).

- **Where:** `Workspace.IKLab` in the place: `IKLab_Quin` + `Targets` (drag balls in Play), `Course`, `IKLabDemo` (client Script; snapshot `studio_snapshot/Workspace/IKLab/IKLabDemo.client.lua`). Three lanes walk out / run back: A animation only, B basic IK, C foot placement + lock + pelvis + slope tilt. Attributes on IKLab: `TimeScale`, `Paused`, `Metrics_A/B/C`.
- **IKControl facts (measured):** works on Bones; no solve in Edit mode; no Pole = knee bends backward; constraints between two bones ARE honoured (hinge hip bone -> knee bone); out-of-reach target = fully straight leg; it overwrites `Bone.Transform` (clip pose unreadable, so bake the clip's foot path); target set in RenderStepped applies one frame late (use `PreAnimation`).
- **Result:** basic IK only stops sinking. Full foot placement cut planted-foot slide at a run from 3.3-3.7 to 0.03-0.05 studs/s and grounded stairs/slopes. Full numbers: `audits/2026-10-03_iklab_ikcontrol_experiment.md`.
- **Verdict given to the owner:** authored animation for the motion, a small foot layer on top; no heavy procedural locomotion. QuinCore already has such a layer ([[quincore-phased-plan-20261001]] pass 13/14, custom solver in ProceduralCombatReactionController).
- **Round 2 (same day, `02f5617`):** four lanes (D = C + knee hinges, head LookAt, bone-attached part, trail, hanging tag); scenarios add strafe, backward, turn in place, circles, jumps; every bone is compared with lane A's (offset, offset speed, reversals/s, acceleration). Foot placement helped in every scenario once "planted" also required the clip foot to be still. Hinge hip bone -> knee bone beats a pole (knee 7-15 deg off the clip vs 16-72). RigidConstraint bone -> part follows exactly. LookAt over-rotates (43-108 deg) and snaps without gating code. A ball-socket hanging part did not follow on anchored rigs. Feet show a tiny 15-55/s flutter (about 0.02 studs per frame), cause not isolated.
- **Round 4 (`9b454ca`):** owner asked for "everything procedural on D, I don't care about computation, then we stress test". Lane D = foot placement + ten switchable layers (Lean, SlopeLean, PelvisSpring, HipTwist, SpineCounter, Look via QuinCore's LookController, ArmLag, Point arm IK, Toes, Breath), attribute `D_<Name>` on Workspace.IKLab, panel bottom-left. Root-level layers move the root before the leg solve; pose-level ones write Bone.Transform in RenderStepped once per animation step (Spine2/Neck/Head are LookController's - don't write them from another layer, its base-reuse guard would compound). Next step the owner expects: the stress test of D (per-layer measurement, many rigs). Not implemented: stride warping, inertial blending, hand contact IK.
- **Round 5 (2026-10-04, `b248d93`):** knee pole placed from the clip's own knee (baked) in C and D: strafe knee 72 -> 17 deg off the clip; that fixed D's thighs, hinge now off by default. StrideWarp (helps only when the body is slower than the clip), Contact (hand on a rail, `Workspace.IKLab.Rails`), Inertial (built, OFF: legs pop because leg IK is solved before any pose edit). Cost: about 35 us script per full Quin per animation step; 65 full Quins = 119 fps. `Crowd` attribute, `Perf` attribute.
- **Round 6 (2026-10-04):** owner confirmed the strafe clips are meant as "move 90 deg left/right, look forward" and that no rename is wanted. Clip facts: hips 63-68 deg toward travel, chest 42-46, head already forward. New D layers: `SquareUp` (chest back to the front, neck counter-turn; 44 -> under 10 deg), `ThighTwist` (leg IK rolls the thigh freely, up to 180 deg off the clip; rolled back using the baked thigh rotation, shin counter-turned; 0 deg RMS), `ArmClear` (elbow/wrist kept 0.95 from the trunk line), `Props` off by default. "Strafe test" programme (`Clip = "#Strafe"`). Owner looks at D by eye and reports what looks wrong (thighs "shy legs", arms through the body): measure that exact thing against lane A before fixing.
- **Round 7 / 7b (2026-10-04):** limb polish on D (Weight footfall, KneeOverToe, SoftElbows, three-spring ArmLag, hand rotation restored under arm IK, REACH_LIMIT 0.995). KneeOverToe first threw the knee round at a run (1021 studs/s) because it also acted on a foot in the air; now fades with foot lift, capped, eased: knee speed equals lane C, inward excess 20 -> 3 deg. The run and walk clips themselves have knees 14-17 deg inside the foot (lane A), so "shy legs" are in the clips. Any rule comparing against a foot/hand direction must be gated to when that direction means something. Owner's next wish: port D's mechanics + the strafe SquareUp into QuinCore (needs a plan; QuinCore has its own foot solver and LookController).
- **Probe when the Studio window is not drawing:** RenderStepped is 0/s, pose-level layers don't run; IK-level results can be read at Heartbeat (bone positions relative to the model pivot, in `task.spawn` + `pcall`, result in an attribute).
- **Measurement trap:** read the drawn pose in the render step, after the pose edits. At `PreAnimation` the engine may have run several animation steps per drawn frame, and the in-between steps show the pose without the edits (looked like a 20-50x head/hand flicker that was never on screen).
- **Owner's question behind round 2:** what can be "planted" on a Quin with little code and just work. Answer given: bone-attached parts, trails/particles/sounds on bones, knee/elbow hinges; LookAt and physics danglers need code or a physics-driven rig.
- **Not tested:** many rigs at once, engine solve cost, arm/hand IK in motion, danglers on a physics-driven Quin, the trail by eye.
