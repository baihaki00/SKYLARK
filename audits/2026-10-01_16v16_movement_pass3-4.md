# 16v16 movement audit — passes 3 and 4 (2026-10-01)

Snapshot of `QUIN_COMBAT.rbxl` scripts after the two movement passes. Method: a server
Heartbeat sampler counting per-frame velocity jumps (>18 studs/s), facing jumps (>20 deg),
teleports and animation restarts, plus a client sampler for foot-IK activity and bone heights;
poses checked by screenshot. Numbers are for the first 57 s of a 16v16 unless stated.

## Pass 3 — locomotion continuity

| Measure | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Velocity jumps, Chase | 206 | 55 |
| Velocity jumps, Recovery | 234 | 7 |
| Velocity jumps, Knockback | 117 | 27 |
| Velocity jumps, Fight | 816 | 358 |
| Animation restart bursts, Chase | 744 | 23 |
| Teleports outside Projectile Jump | 67 | 2 |

Causes fixed: gait stopped by every action overlay and on every Chase exit/entry; steering and
acceleration advanced only on the 10 Hz state tick; Fall pose owned by two systems; ground
overlays starting in mid-air (undefined `isFreefallState`); jump clip not fitted to flight time;
de-stacking teleporting Quins passing overhead; uncapped knockback launches; bouncing collision
body; re-entry leaps aimed at the world origin (arena centre is (0, 0, -438)); `tick()` vs
`os.clock()` mix-ups disabling dash / slide / wall-run cooldowns; dash overshooting its target;
sprint arrival overshooting melee range; slides and lunges switching on and off instantly.

Switched off behind `CombatConfig` flags (clips are not usable as authored):
`Chase_TurnCutOverlayEnabled`, `Chase_ArcRunOverlayEnabled`, `Turn180PivotClipEnabled`.

## Pass 4 — rig, IK, recovery, wall-run, projectile jump

- **QuinFemale model scale 0.044** made the Animator shrink all clip hip translation to 4%.
  Templates rebuilt as scale-1 models (in the place file, not in this repo). Hip height during
  get-up: 4.44 -> 2.12 studs (male 1.95).
- **Get-up**: `GetUpGround` pointed at the same asset as `GetUpBackFast`. Heavy knockdowns now use
  `79207866638803`; Recovery lasts as long as its clip.
- **Foot IK** measured the floor against 5.36 studs; the rig floats at 5.11. IK active
  33-53% of frames -> 0.8%; enable/disable toggles down about 20x.
- **Air knockback pose**: fixed-target arm/leg IK (T-pose) replaced by the authored clip plus a
  velocity-driven whole-body lean.
- **Wall-run**: requires 24 studs of wall ahead, climbs 3.5 studs, holds distance, body leans
  away from the wall. Still rare in play (0-1 per 75-160 s).
- **Projectile jump**: landing scatter rolled once per jump; touchdown, strafe end and arena
  containment checked every frame; dive instead of teleport when impact is reached in the air.
  Time on the ground before impact 0.5 s -> <=0.06 s; landing snap up to 266 studs -> <=2.2.
