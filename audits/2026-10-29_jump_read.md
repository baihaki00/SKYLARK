# Jump read: a ground Quin reads an enemy's projectile jump (2026-10-29)

The owner asked: can a Quin on the ground track a PJ, read whether it is about to dive at it, ignore it and pick another target, or intercept it in the air, by its own choice?

## What there was

- **`AirInterceptModule`:** a Quin that sees an enemy 40+ studs above it may go up and meet it (mid-air clash). One per jumper, more often for aggressive Quins. Called from Overwatch and Chase's climb.
- **`FightState.Decide`:** only when its own target is in a PJ. It picks meet / block / dodge at random, **when the jump starts**, seconds before the dive arrives. The block lasts 0.45 s and the dodge 0.35 s, both long over by the landing.
- **Nothing** works out where or when a jump lands, or whether it is aimed at this Quin.

How the dive lands (`ProjectileJumpState/Flight.calculateCombatAimPoint`):
- It aims at its target's position, led by the target's speed (0.04-0.4 s).
- It pulls back 4.5 studs toward the jumper, then scatters 5-25 studs (12% of the jump's length).
- It re-aims every frame until it is within 10 studs of the aim point, at `ProjectileJump_SlamSpeed` 480.
- The landing knocks down enemies within 6 studs (`SlamKnockdownRadius`) and shoves those within 14 (`SlamShockwaveRadius`).

## Built: `Modules/JumpRead`

**Reading the landing** (`JumpRead.predict`):
- **Diving** (Dash / Strafe / ComboStrafe / Impact, falling): along its velocity to the floor, plus how soon (distance ÷ speed).
- **Still up:** near the jumper's target, give or take the scatter (5-25 studs). When: "later".

A Quin reacts only to a jumper it can see (Cognition contact). It counts as a threat if the jump is aimed at it, or it lands within the slam plus the spread.

**The choice, once per jump, by temperament:**

| Choice | Leaning | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| clear | 0.5 + DefensePreference | runs out to 4 studs past the slam's edge (40 studs/s), then carries on |
| punish | 0.2 + Aggression (jumper within 80 studs) | clears, then once the jumper has landed: targets it and chases it (`PunishLanding`) |
| intercept | 0.6 × Aggression (still rising, 40+ studs above, PJ ready, not already claimed) | `AirInterceptModule.commit` + the intercept jump |
| ignore | 0.35 × Aggression, never for a jump aimed at it | stays on its own fight |

**Where it runs:**
- `Main`, each tick, for a standing Quin on the ground. It works like the headroom escape: while it clears, the state holds.
- A jump aimed at this Quin is cleared only once the dive has committed. Before that, the dive keeps steering onto it.

**Switches:**
- `CombatConfig.JumpRead_Enabled`, or Workspace `JumpReadOff = true`.
- Off: the old random reaction in `FightState.Decide` comes back. On, Decide skips it.
- Tunables: `JumpRead_ClearSpeed`, `_ClearMargin`, `_PunishRange`, `_InterceptWeight`, `_IgnoreWeight`.

**Readouts:**
- `JumpRead`, e.g. "Quin_X at me: clear, lands in 0.4 s, 9 studs off".
- `JumpReadChoice`, `JumpReadAt`.
- `ObstacleAwareness` "Clearing out: a jump lands here".
- `JumpRead.stats` counts.

## What this shows

1. **Getting clear does not save the jump's own target.** Over both runs the target was slammed in 7/42 (17%) of landings with the read on, against 10/56 (18%) with it off. The dive re-aims at its target every frame until 10 studs from its aim point, so running away is followed. Bystanders do get clear; how often they were saved is not measured.
2. **Punish cannot hit, by rule.** `FightState` does not strike a target in Knockback, Airborne or Recovery, or one with `GetUpProtection` (set by `RecoveryState`). A jump's landing is played in Recovery, so the jumper is protected through its whole landing. Punishers get there (7) but can't hit.
3. **Intercept is rare** (2 picks). Most jumps are first seen already diving or too low.

## Owner calls

- **Is a jump's own landing punishable?** For example, no `GetUpProtection` and strikes allowed while a PJ's landing clip plays. Today it counts as getting up.
- **The target's own escape:** a last-moment sidestep (a focus dash or quick evade when the dive is ~0.1 s out) instead of running. That's the human read of a tracking dive. Possible next step.

## A jump's landing made punishable (owner: "Yes, punishable")

`RecoveryState.enter`:
- After a projectile jump's landing (`KnockbackType` `slam_landing` or `traversal_landing`), it sets `PJLandingOpen` and does **not** set `GetUpProtection`.
- `PJLandingOpen` is cleared on exit.

What reads it:
- `DamageModule`: the "GetUpProtected" refusal skips a Recovery with `PJLandingOpen`.
- `FightState`: the downed check (no strikes, the downed reaction) skips it too.
- `CirclingState`: so does `isTargetDown`.

Switch: `CombatConfig.PJLanding_Punishable = false`, or Workspace `PJLandingProtectOn = true` (protected, as before).

**A/B**, landing open vs protected, 20 s windows, jump read on. A strike is a jumper's `ImpactType` going LIGHT/HEAVY within 1.5 s of landing:

|---|---|---|
| Landings | 31 | 25 |
| Struck within 1.5 s | **1** | 0 |
| Knocked down within 1.5 s (earlier run) | 2 of 44 | 0 of 38 |

No script errors. The run before used the `Health` attribute (0 / 0), which is not what a hit lowers: strikes go to `Humanoid:TakeDamage`.

**Still almost no punishes.** Punishers reach the jumper (8 of 17 within 10 studs) but don't strike in time. Untraced: the trace run hit the end of the match, and then Studio's Play start hung again. Likely causes:

## Why punishers didn't strike (trace, 100 s)

11 punishes, 6 got within 10 studs, **0 struck**. In 4 of the 7 traced, the brain's next target pick (≈0.1 s after the punisher went in) turned it to another enemy. A landing in the open lasted 0.5-0.9 s in these traces: there was time.

**Fixes** (`JumpRead`, `ChaseState`):
- A punisher holds the jumper through `TargetOverride` for `JumpRead_PunishCommit` (1.5 s). It is cleared only if it is still that jumper, so the social layer's own overrides are untouched.
- Already in striking range (≤ 1.5 × CombatRange), it goes straight to Fight; otherwise Chase.
- Chase in range of a jumper with `PJLandingOpen` goes to Fight, not the 30% Circling.

## The target's last-moment sidestep (owner: "Build the sidestep")

**Why:** running doesn't save a dive's own target (17% vs 18%). The dive re-aims every frame until it is 10 studs from its aim point.

**What it does:**
- A Quin the dive is aimed at no longer runs once the dive has committed. If a focus dash is ready, it times the dive frame by frame (`JumpRead.watchDive`, a Heartbeat, ended by the jump ending or after 3 s).
- When the dive's predicted touchdown is `JumpRead_SidestepLead` (0.2 s) away, it dashes across the dive line, to the side away from where the dive is landing.
- The dash is `Evasion.focusDash` with `curve = 0` and `anyState`: it runs in any standing state, not only Fight / Circling. Main holds the state while the dash has the body.
- With no dash ready, it runs as before.

Switch: Workspace `JumpSidestepOff = true`, or `CombatConfig.JumpRead_Sidestep = false`. The watcher rechecks it every frame (a watch started while on fired after the switch went off; fixed).

Readouts: `JumpSidestep` (dash kind), `JumpSidestepAt`.

**A/B, two runs, 20 s windows** (the second run was cut short by the match ending):

| | On | Off |
|---|---|---|
| Dive landings on a ground target | 27 | 41 |
| Target slammed | **0** | 3 (7%) |
| Landings where the target sidestepped | 5 (0 slammed) | n/a |
| Sidesteps fired | 14 | (6, the switch bug above) |

No script errors.

**What it shows:** no slam when the target sidestepped, and no harm seen. But slams on a dive's own target are rare to begin with (7% with it off), and only 5 landings had a sidestep, so this needs the owner's eye more than more numbers.

What to watch:
- the dash fires about 0.2 s before touchdown;
- the jumper lands beside the Quin, not on it;
- whether 0.2 s reads as a reaction or as too late / too early.
