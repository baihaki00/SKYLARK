## Deficiencies (observed → verified cause)

### M2-1. Play As Quin still takes over tournament fighters (user item 1)
- Observed: at 39.3s the player's `Character` became `Quin_Male_BB2E` (QuinServer, TeamAlpha,
  tagged `Quin`); released at 42.3s; Team Alpha fought the rest of the match with 15.
- Cause (verified): `ServerScriptService/Server.server.lua` `Possess` is the pre-costume
  version again — explicit/spectated target, else the first living Quin in QuinServer, else any
  tagged Quin; `Release` releases by `ControllingPlayer`. The costume version of Possess/Release
  from the earlier fix is not in the place. The `HitboxModule` same-world filter and the
  orchestrator's `getTournamentFighters()` (excludes costumes) are present.
- What triggered the possess at 39.3s (P key vs HUD button): UNKNOWN (not instrumented).

### M2-2. Element colour invisible on Male Quins (user item 2)
- Cause (verified): `FXService.applyElementAppearance` was changed to "preserve skin textures"
  — it sets `Alpha_Surface.Color` but no longer clears `TextureID`. `QuinMale.Alpha_Surface`
  has a TextureID, which overrides `Color`, so Males show no element tint. `QuinFemale` has no
  texture and does tint. The function also deletes `ElementHighlight` and never recreates it.
- `ReplicatedStorage.QuinType` still contains `QuinTypeA`–`QuinTypeD` next to `QuinMale` /
  `QuinFemale`; `Possess`'s fallback still spawns `"TypeA"` (user item 3).

### M2-3. Retreat crashes to Idle
- Observed: 8 errors `RetreatState:214/301: attempt to index nil with 'setTarget'`; retreat
  exits: Idle 21 (crash fallback), Knockback 9 of 30.
- Cause (verified): RetreatState was changed to call `TargetingModule.setTarget(...)` but the
  script does not require `TargetingModule`.

### M2-4. Hits land without a visible attack (user item 7)
- Observed: in 562 of 574 knockbacks, the nearest enemy (≤18 studs) was not showing any
  Action-or-higher clip with weight > 0.3 at that instant; that enemy was in Fight 464 times.
  12 knockbacks had no enemy within 18 studs at all.
- Cause: UNKNOWN. Nearest enemy is a proxy for the attacker; the damage/knockback call path
  (who calls `KnockbackModule.apply` and when relative to the attack clip) has not been traced
  yet. Fight shows `Attacks.Punches.Hook|Tactics.DesperateCounter` (same asset id) 38% of
  Fight time, so attack clips do play — but apparently not at the moment hits land.
- Related: 0 deaths in 240s of 16v16 combat.

### M2-5. Quins pinned in place while "running"
- Observed: 1026 stuck samples (Chase 465+, Circling 164+ at the 155s mark). Live probe of
  pinned Quins: WalkSpeed 10–50, `MoveDirection` magnitude 1, Humanoid state Running, floor
  ArenaGround, no wall within 6 studs ahead, no Quin within 5 studs, no foreign joints, no
  anchored parts, no overlapping parts, stun expired — velocity 0.0 both before and after the
  physics step. Disabling the gyros for 0.6s did not free it (0.2 studs moved).
- Cause: UNKNOWN. Not the `Main` inert branch (that sets WalkSpeed 0). Needs a controlled
  single-Quin repro.

### M2-6. Leaked orientation constraints
- Observed: pinned/moving Quins in Chase and Circling carry `FightGyro` (AlignOrientation,
  60000 torque) created by FightState; Circling Quins carry both `FightGyro` and
  `CirclingGyro`. Combined with `steer()` re-enabling `AutoRotate`, three systems drive facing.
- Cause (verified by inspection of live instances): FightGyro is not removed on Fight exit.

### M2-7. Chase is jumpy, stop-start, and often shows no locomotion (user items 5, 6, 9)
- Observed: 1090 airborne starts in Chase in ~4 minutes; Jump/Vault clip shown 14% of Chase
  time; plain idle shown 22% of Chase time; walk clip 14%; push-off clips 19%; stop/start
  22.4 per Quin-minute; 57s of run loop visible while airborne.
- Cause: not traced yet (jump triggers in Chase: obstacle vault / elevated target / stuck
  unstick branches are candidates — the stuck rate in M2-5 would feed the unstick jump).

### M2-8. Commitment
- Fight↔Chase / Fight↔Circling flips remain the dominant pattern (211 of 250). Idle still 16%.
- Target changes 7.4 per Quin-minute with 107 quick flip-backs.

### M2-9. Circling
- Clip direction now matches lateral motion most of the time (left/left 459s, right/right
  439s), but radius is still 10–37 studs and 36% of circling speed is radial; Circling shows
  `StrafeLeftWalk|RetreatBackstep` (shared asset id) 30%.
