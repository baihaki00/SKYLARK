# Run leap: a running long jump (2026-10-12)

**What the owner asked for:** a "super long athletic jump" in the middle of a run, with a little running in the air, then straight back to running with no stop.

## The clips
- **How they were made:** `scripts/make_run_leap.lua` built them from the Quins' own run (`Movement.Run`, 109090784752055, a 0.467 s cycle). It walks the run's phase:
  1. starts at the push-off pose;
  2. widens to a split with the legs and arms exaggerated around the cycle's average pose (×1.7 at the hang);
  3. lingers there;
  4. does one quick air cycle;
  5. ends on the run's exact foot-contact pose.
- **The owner published:**
  - `Movement.RunLeapRight` = 77977577669807 (off the right foot, lands on the left);
  - `Movement.RunLeapLeft` = 134666516301359 (off the left, lands on the right).
- **Each clip:** 0.9 s, with markers `Jump` and `Footstep`.
- **Preview strip:** `2026-10-12_run_leap_strip.jpg`.

## Wiring (LocomotionModule.jump, for the player and the AI)

**When the leap plays.** `runLeapFor` replaces the jump clip when all of these hold:
- the jump type is free, long jump or hurdle;
- the flight is at least 0.35 s;
- the body is at 85 % or more of its top speed;
- the run clip is playing.

**Which foot.** It is read from the run's phase **before** the launch releases the run loop. (The first try read it after, so it never fired.)

**In the air.** Every frame, the time left to come back down to the takeoff height sets the clip's rate (0.6–2.6). A tapped jump (a cut jump) and a held jump both reach the contact pose at touchdown.

**At touchdown.** The clip fades out over 0.12 s, and `Movement.Run` is put at the contact the leap lands on: left 0.167, right 0.433.

**Debug attributes.** `RunLeap` is set during a leap; `RunLeapSkip` holds the reason a jump did not leap.

**Tunables (CombatConfig).**
- `Locomotion_RunLeap` (switch)
- `…MinSpeedShare`
- `…MinFlight`
- `…RateMin/Max`
- `…LandFade`
- `…LeftContactTime` / `…RightContactTime`
- `…RightContactPhase`
