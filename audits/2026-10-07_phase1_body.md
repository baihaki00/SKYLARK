# Phase 1: The body (agile grip, stamina by effort, free and engaged movement, head)

Part of [QUIN_CREATURE_DESIGN.md](../QUIN_CREATURE_DESIGN.md), phase 1. Every change is for every Quin, AI and player alike, and each one sits behind a switch in CombatConfig.

## What changed

| Piece | What | Switch |
|---|---|---|
| **Agile body** (`LocomotionModule.tune`) | Lateral grip 90 → **200** studs/s² (≈ 0.46 g → ≈ 1 g). Sprint turn-rate cap 2.2 → 6 rad/s, so the grip decides the turn instead of the cap. Reversal: brake 150 → 220, pivot speed 6 → 8, pivot turn 7 → 11 rad/s. | `Body_Agile` (the `*_Agile` values) |
| **Stamina by effort** (`Modules/BodyStamina`, called by Main for every Quin) | Running hard (≥ 80 % of its own top speed) costs `EnergyDrain_Sprint`/s. Moving slower recovers `EnergyRecovery_Walk`/s; standing recovers `EnergyRecovery_Idle`/s. Nothing changes in the air, knocked down or mid-strike. The per-state code (Chase drain/recover, Idle / Circling / Overwatch recover) only runs with the switch off. | `Body_StaminaByEffort` |
| **Head leads a turn** (`LookController`, `SteerIntent`) | The steer publishes where the body means to go (at most 5 times a second, on changes of 15° or more). Running at more than 12 studs/s into a turn of 20° or more, the head, neck and chest look there first. | `Body_HeadLeadsTurn` |
| **The player's view is the Quin's sight** | Your piloted Quin's head follows your camera, within neck limits. Other clients see it via `PilotLookYaw` / `PilotLookPitch`, which your client sends at most 5 times a second. | — |
| **Free and engaged movement** (player, `PilotedState`) | An enemy within `EngageRange` (16 studs; it lets go at 20) and you not running: the body squares up to it (the fight's facing gyro). S steps back, A/D circle; the gait picks strafe or backwards from the motion. Otherwise it moves free: the body faces where it goes. | `PlayerQuin.EngageRange` |

## Reading

- **The agile body keeps 1v1 fights together.** About 40 % more strikes, more time in contact, far less time far apart. Hit rate is unchanged and whiffs are fewer.
- **The 16v16 barely moves.** It has many fights and targets changing, so the numbers average out.
- **To watch:**
  - the 1v1 is denser and its rhythm a bit smoother (fewer swings a minute), which is what phase 5's breaths are for;
  - projectile jumps are a little rarer, likely because circling no longer recovers 30 mana a second (the old Circling recovery); stamina is now by effort.
- **Momentum into a fight is unchanged.** That is phase 2.
