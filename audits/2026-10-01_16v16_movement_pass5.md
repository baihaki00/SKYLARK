# 16v16 movement audit — pass 5 (2026-10-01): legs vs ground

Scope: the owner's list after passes 3-4 — shuffling, Circling, walking by choice, GASP removal,
landing clips, slides. Studio backup: `ServerStorage.backup_pre_16v16audit5_20261001`.

## How it was measured

- **Clip ground speed** (Edit mode, rig clone, `Animator:StepAnimations`): the root velocity that
  keeps the stance foot still, from the toe's travel in root space over the stance phase.
  Two estimators disagree by about 15%, so treat single values as +/-15%.

  | Clip | Ground speed at 1.0x | Direction |
  |---|---|---|
  | Walk | 6.4 - 7.4 | forward |
  | Jog | 8.2 - 10.4 | forward |
  | Run | 28.7 - 31.6 | forward |
  | StrafeWalk L/R | 4.6 - 6.5 | exactly +/-90 deg |
  | StrafeRun L/R | 17.8 - 18.3 | exactly +/-90 deg |
  | StrafeTired L/R | 0.5 - 1.0, with 3.5 studs of foot travel per cycle | drags both feet |

- **Foot slip in play** (client RenderStepped): horizontal speed of the foot that is on the floor
  (lower of ankle / toe speed, both frames within 0.2 of the floor), grouped by state, root speed
  and leg clip. Reliable for walking and strafing; at sprint the stance is 4 frames long, so the
  number is an upper bound.

## Causes found and fixed

| Cause | Fix |
|---|---|
| Circling moved at 10 / 20.5 / 30 studs/s under clips authored for 2.6 / 7.3 / 18.5 | Pace is the clip's own ground speed; play rate follows measured sideways speed |
| Strafe clips only step sideways, Circling moved diagonally | Body turns up to `Circling_MaxFacingBias` so motion is exactly lateral; other directions face travel and use the gait |
| Strafe clip kept cycling while standing | Stopped below 25% of clip speed |
| StrafeTired drags both feet at any pace | Tired tension walks the StrafeWalk clip at `Strafe_TiredPace` (0.65) |
| Arriving in Circling at sprint speed under a walk-strafe clip | Momentum (too fast, or not yet along the path) is run off facing travel with the gait |
| Gait kept running in place ~0.5 s after every stop (released only by a 0.35 s orphan timer) | Gait follows velocity down and releases below 0.4 studs/s |
| Start-run clip (0.7 s, Action priority) covered the legs for its full length; body reached sprint in 0.5 s. About a third of Chase time | Handed over to the gait with a 0.2 s fade when the push-off ends |
| Gait speed lagged the body by ~8 studs/s through every acceleration; Jog clip clamped at 1.5x between 12.6 and 26 studs/s | Follow rate 12 -> 25 /s; Run blends in from 11 to 18 studs/s |
| Run clip speed configured 26.8, measured ~29.5 | `Gait_RunAuthoredSpeed = 29.5` |
| AI "walk" was 16 studs/s (a jog), sprint 50 exceeded what the Run clip can cover | Walk 7.5, `Speed` 40 |
| Rest to sprint in 0.5 s | `Locomotion_Acceleration` 80 -> 55 (0.7 s) |
| HipHeight 3.8 sank the feet ~0.3 studs | HipHeight derived from the rig's sole depth at spawn |
| Soft landing clip played while the Quin kept sprinting (glide in a crouch) | Dismount landing brakes through the crouch, clip released as the body rises |
